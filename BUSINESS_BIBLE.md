PART 1: BUSINESS BIBLE
1. Executive Summary
ItuwaAfrica is an independent African AI education platform designed to help people learn practical artificial intelligence skills, build demonstrable projects, and develop the ability to offer valuable digital services.
The platform addresses a growing need for accessible, affordable and practical AI education across Africa.
It is designed primarily for aspiring freelancers and existing digital professionals who want to use AI to improve their skills, create services, become more productive and compete in the digital economy.
Unlike a traditional online course marketplace, the platform focuses on guided learning, structured challenges, practical assignments, portfolio development and service monetization.
The business starts in Nigeria and expands into other African markets as product-market fit, payment coverage and content partnerships develop.

6. Customer Transformation
The platform should move customers through five stages:
Stage 1: Awareness
The learner understands what AI can do.
Stage 2: Knowledge
The learner understands selected AI tools and how to apply them to practical work.
Stage 3: Practical competence
The learner can complete useful tasks independently.
Stage 4: Demonstrated ability
The learner has completed projects and built a portfolio.
Stage 5: Commercial readiness
The learner understands how to package and deliver a digital service.
The platform provides education and guidance. It does not guarantee customers, income or employment.

8. Learning Methodology
Every course should follow a repeatable learning framework.
Explain: Teach the concept in simple language.
Demonstrate: Show a real-world example.
Practice: Give the learner a guided task.
Build: Require an independent practical output.
Review: Evaluate the result against clear criteria.
Apply: Explain how the skill can be used professionally.
Course structure:
Academy → Course → Module → Lesson → Exercise → Assessment → Project.
Each course should have measurable learning outcomes.
Recommended lesson duration: 3–8 minutes for most video lessons.
Longer demonstrations are permitted where the subject requires them.
Written lessons and downloadable resources should accompany important video content whenever practical.

17. Major Risks
Risk: Learners register but do not complete courses.
Response: Short lessons, challenges, progress tracking and accountability.
Risk: Free YouTube content reduces willingness to pay.
Response: Sell structured learning, projects, feedback and guided pathways.
Risk: AI tools change rapidly.
Response: Review and update courses regularly.
Risk: Payment failures.
Response: Support multiple payment providers where commercially viable.
Risk: High video delivery costs.
Response: Compress videos, use suitable video hosting, provide written alternatives and monitor bandwidth.
Risk: Learners expect guaranteed income.
Response: Communicate realistic outcomes and distinguish education from employment placement.
Risk: Subscription cancellations.
Response: Provide ongoing value, transparent renewal and easy cancellation.
18. Non-Negotiable Business Principles
