# MASTER PROMPT: ituwaAfrica Complete Product Copy & Messaging Rewrite

## ROLE

Act as a senior product positioning strategist, conversion copywriter, UX writer and product-minded frontend engineer.

You are working on **ituwaAfrica**, an Africa-focused practical digital skills learning platform.

Your task is to audit and rewrite the entire application's user-facing copy so that its product offering is immediately understandable, honest, trustworthy and extremely simple.

This is a product-wide copy transformation, not a homepage redesign.

Do not simply replace a few headlines. Inspect the entire codebase, understand the existing product, identify every user-facing text string and improve the messaging consistently across all routes, components and user journeys.

## 1. BUSINESS CONTEXT

ituwaAfrica teaches practical digital skills through free foundations and paid specialist courses:

1. AI Foundations
2. AI Content Marketing for Business
3. AI App Development for Business
4. Creator Economy & YouTube
5. AI Video Creation
6. AI Graphic Design

The primary audience is freelancers and aspiring freelancers in Nigeria and across Africa, including students, working professionals, creators and people exploring digital skills.

The platform should be accessible to beginners and people with limited technical experience.

The business has two distinct offerings:

### OFFER A: FREE LEARNING

Free learning is a core part of the business model, not a temporary marketing gimmick.

People should be able to:

- Learn through free YouTube tutorials.
- Access available free courses on ituwaAfrica.
- Explore the available practical skill areas.
- Follow practical lessons.
- Complete exercises and projects.
- Understand AI tools and their limitations.
- Develop useful digital skills at their own pace.

The free content must be genuinely useful.

Never write copy that makes free learning sound inferior, deliberately incomplete or designed to frustrate people into paying.

Do not imply that someone must purchase mentorship to gain useful knowledge.

### OFFER B: PAID SPECIALIST COURSES

The paid catalogue includes specialist courses in AI video creation, AI content marketing for business, AI app development for business, YouTube monetisation and AI graphic design.

AI Content Marketing for Business and AI App Development for Business are exclusive specialist courses. Their lessons are in development; do not imply that they are already available to study or promise a release date.

Describe course prices and availability accurately from the course catalogue. Payment does not guarantee clients, employment, audience growth, platform monetisation or income.

### OFFER C: PERSONAL MENTORSHIP

Personal mentorship is a separate, one-on-one service with the founder.

People pay because they want more than general information.

They may need:

- Help choosing the right learning direction.
- Personal guidance applying a skill.
- Feedback on their projects.
- Help identifying and correcting mistakes.
- Guidance building a practical solution.
- Support moving from learning into implementation.

Position this as personal mentorship and guided implementation, not simply access to more recorded courses.

The copy must explain what the learner receives, what the engagement covers and what it does not cover.

Do not invent session durations, prices, availability, guarantees, unlimited access or deliverables. If those details are not defined in the application or business configuration, use neutral wording or clearly mark them for the owner to complete.

Do not suggest that mentorship guarantees clients, employment, subscribers, monetisation or income.

## 2. CORE POSITIONING

The central message should be:

**Learn digital skills. Build real things. Get personal guidance when you need it.**

Supporting explanation:

"ituwaAfrica helps businesses, freelancers, creators and individuals build practical AI skills through lessons and projects. Start with free AI foundations, then explore specialist courses in content marketing, app development, video creation, YouTube and graphic design."

This positioning must be reflected throughout the product.

A visitor should understand within five seconds:

1. What ituwaAfrica is.
2. What they can learn.
3. What they can access for free.
4. What paid specialist courses offer.
5. What personal mentorship means.

## 3. IMPORTANT AUDIENCE AND CULTURAL CONTEXT

The initial market is Nigeria, with wider African expansion.

Write for people who may:

- Be new to AI and digital tools.
- Have limited access to expensive software.
- Use Android phones as their primary devices.
- Have inconsistent internet connectivity.
- Be cautious because of exaggerated online income promises.
- Want practical skills they can apply to real work.
- Need affordable learning options.
- Be unsure which digital skill to learn first.

Do not stereotype Nigerian users.

Do not use slang, exaggerated motivational language, get-rich-quick language or fake urgency.

Use clear, natural international English that feels familiar to Nigerian and African readers.

Use familiar words instead of unnecessary technical jargon.

Where technical terms are necessary, explain them simply.

## 4. NON-NEGOTIABLE COPY RULES

### Be specific

Every headline, paragraph, course description and CTA should communicate a clear idea.

Prefer:
- Plan a useful business campaign with AI assistance.
- Learn to create professional content with AI.
- Plan your first YouTube channel.
- Create a freelance service proposal.
- Build and test a simple AI app.

Avoid:
- Unlock your potential.
- Become unstoppable.
- Transform your life.
- Achieve financial freedom.
- Master the future.
- Turn your dreams into reality.

### No exaggerated promises

NEVER promise or imply:

- Guaranteed income.
- Guaranteed clients.
- Guaranteed employment.
- Guaranteed YouTube monetisation.
- Guaranteed subscribers or views.
- Guaranteed business success.
- Making dollars within a specified period.
- Becoming an expert after completing a course.

Do not invent testimonials, reviews, learner statistics, success stories, revenue figures or performance data.

### Do not attack free tutorials

The founder intends to grow through free YouTube tutorials.

Never use messaging such as:

"Most AI tutorials teach you nothing useful."

Instead, communicate:

"Watching a tutorial is a good place to start. Practising what you learn is how you build a skill."

Respect free education. ituwaAfrica should be seen as a place that teaches openly and honestly.

### Explain the difference between free learning and paid mentorship

Never confuse free course access with paid personal mentorship.

A visitor must understand that:

- Free learning provides education and practical exercises.
- Paid mentorship provides personal guidance from the founder.
- Paying does not buy guaranteed business or financial results.

### Avoid vague product language

Reduce unnecessary use of:
- Ecosystem.
- Transformation.
- Empowerment.
- Revolutionary.
- Cutting-edge.
- Unlock.
- AI-powered future.
- Premium experience.
- Limitless potential.

Use direct descriptions instead.

## 5. COURSE AND SKILL-AREA MESSAGING

Keep the six current skill areas aligned with the course catalogue.

Use simple descriptions:

**AI Foundations**
Understand AI and learn how to use it for everyday tasks.

**AI Content Marketing for Business**
Research an audience and create useful, original content tied to a business goal.

**AI App Development for Business**
Identify a real customer problem, build a focused app prototype and test its value.

**Creator Economy & YouTube**
Create original work, understand platform policies and explore realistic creator revenue paths.

**AI Freelancing**
Learn how to package and present digital services to clients.

**AI Video Creation and AI Graphic Design**
Create original visual work with AI-assisted tools, with attention to audience value and rights.

Important YouTube structure:

- Video production and visual design are distinct skill areas from YouTube channel strategy and creator business.
- Explain current policy requirements accurately; never imply that course completion guarantees monetisation.

Do not merge these academies.

## 6. COURSE COPY REQUIREMENTS

Rewrite every course title, summary, description, learning outcome and project description where necessary.

Every course should explain:

1. Who the course is for.
2. What problem it helps solve.
3. What the learner will learn.
4. What the learner will make or complete.
5. What tools or equipment are needed.
6. What the course does not guarantee, where relevant.

Prioritise tangible outputs over aspirational promises.

Examples:

Instead of:
"Learn AI for your freelance business."

Prefer:
"Create a freelance service offer, prepare a proposal and practise responding to a client brief."

Instead of:
"Build a profitable YouTube channel."

Prefer:
"Choose a channel topic, plan your first videos and create a realistic publishing schedule."

Instead of:
"Earn money quickly by using AI."

Prefer:
"Build a practical campaign sample and learn how it can support a business goal. Results depend on the audience, offer and execution."

For YouTube courses, maintain accurate explanations of copyright, reused content, monetisation policies and YouTube Partner Program eligibility.

Completing a course must never be presented as a guarantee of YPP approval or income.

Rewrite the homepage around this messaging hierarchy:

1. Simple explanation of ituwaAfrica.
2. Clear primary CTA to start free learning.
3. Explanation of the free starting course and specialist courses.
4. What learners can make or practise.
5. How free learning works.
6. Personal mentorship explanation.
7. Trust and transparency section.
8. Clear final CTA.

Recommended hero direction:

Headline:
"Learn digital skills. Build real things."

Supporting copy:
"Learn practical AI skills for business, freelance and creator work. Start with free foundations, then explore specialist courses in content marketing, app development, video creation, YouTube and graphic design."

Primary CTA:
"Start learning free"

Secondary CTA:
"Explore the academies"

Use these as starting points. Improve the wording if a better, simpler version communicates the same positioning.

Do not force every academy, benefit or business detail into the hero.

CTA direction:

Only show a booking or application CTA if the corresponding functionality exists.

## 10. TRUST AND TRANSPARENCY

Remove or correct any fake, unsupported or placeholder claims.

Examples to inspect:

- ₦50K–₦250K per brief.
- 4+ hours reclaimed.
- +18.4% learner growth.
- 86% active learners.
- High retention.
- All systems operational.
- Any testimonials or results that are not verified.

If a metric is dynamic, connect it to its actual data source.

If it is placeholder content, remove it or label it clearly as sample data.

Do not create fake testimonials or success stories.

Ensure legal copy does not claim security, encryption, data protection or infrastructure capabilities that have not been verified in the actual implementation.

Do not rewrite legal clauses in a way that changes their legal meaning without flagging the change for review.

## 13. IMPORTANT TECHNICAL CONSTRAINTS

Before changing anything:

1. Inspect the project structure.
2. Read the existing README and AGENTS.md, if available.
3. Inspect the routes, components and content models.
4. Identify where copy is stored.
5. Identify whether content is static, database-driven or configured through shared constants.
6. Understand current access-control and enrolment behaviour.

Then implement the copy changes in the appropriate source locations.

Do not:

- Delete existing functionality.
- Break routes.
- Change database schemas unnecessarily.
- Change Supabase authentication.
- Change Row Level Security policies.
- Change payment logic.
- Change course enrolment rules without explicit approval.
- Introduce fake pricing.
- Replace dynamic data with hardcoded claims.
- Break mobile layouts.
- Invent mentorship booking functionality.
- Change existing academy relationships.
- Remove existing courses.

If the current implementation contradicts the intended free-learning and paid-mentorship model, identify the discrepancy and explain it instead of silently changing business logic.

Keep the code clean and avoid duplicating copy in multiple places when a shared source of truth is appropriate.

## FINAL STANDARD

The entire application must communicate one simple idea:

**ituwaAfrica helps people learn practical digital skills for free, practise what they learn, and get personal mentorship when they need deeper guidance.**

A visitor should never need to guess what we teach, what is free, why mentorship costs money or what they will receive.

Make it so simple that a Nigerian teenager can understand the offering, explain it to a friend and know exactly what to do next.

Clarity before cleverness. Honesty before hype. Practical outcomes before promises.
