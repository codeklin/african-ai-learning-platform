# Module 1: Planning an Original Video

---

### Lesson 1.1: The Purpose of Your Video and Who It Is For
- **Estimated Video Length**: 3 minutes 30 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-1`
- **Main Skill**: Identify a clear video objective and target audience before generating any AI clips.
- **Key Principle**: Never generate random pretty videos. Pick one specific question to answer or one story to tell.

---

### Lesson 1.2: Writing a Short 45-Second Hook-and-Hold Script
- **Estimated Video Length**: 4 minutes 15 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-2`
- **Main Skill**: Structure a 3-part script optimized for vertical retention (Shorts / Reels / TikTok).
- **The 45-Second Script Structure**:
  1. The Hook (0–4 seconds / ~10 words): Stop the thumb scroll.
  2. The Core Insight (5–35 seconds / ~60 words): Deliver the value in 3 distinct beats.
  3. The Takeaway & CTA (36–45 seconds / ~20 words): One clear lesson and action.
- **Copyable Script Prompt**:
  ```text
  Act as a short-form video scriptwriter for TikTok and Instagram Reels.
  Topic: 3 simple daily habits that protect your laptop battery in hot Nigerian weather.
  Target audience: Students and remote workers.
  Write a word-for-word spoken script that takes exactly 45 seconds to speak (around 90 to 100 words total).
  Structure:
  - Hook (0-5s): A punchy opening that calls out overheating laptops.
  - Beat 1, 2, 3: Practical steps (avoid bedsheets, charge between 20-80%, keep out of direct car sunlight).
  - Outro (40-45s): Follow for practical tech tips.
  Do not include stage directions in the dialogue text.
  ```

---

### Lesson 1.3: Storyboarding and Scene Planning
- **Estimated Video Length**: 3 minutes 45 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-3`
- **Main Skill**: Break a script into 5 to 7 specific visual scenes before opening an AI generator.
- **Storyboard Template**:
  - Scene 1 (0–5s): Close-up shot of laptop vents blocked by a bedsheet.
  - Scene 2 (5–15s): Overheating battery indicator warning on a phone/laptop screen.
  - Scene 3 (15–25s): Laptop sitting elevated on a clean wooden desk with airflow.
  - Scene 4 (25–35s): Battery gauge showing 80% charging pause.
  - Scene 5 (35–45s): Smiling creator working smoothly at a desk.

---

### Module 1 Quiz
1. **Why is it essential to write a script and storyboard before generating AI video clips?**
   - Correct: Because generating clips without a plan wastes time and free credits on random visuals that do not fit together.
2. **What is the ideal word count for a 45-second spoken short-form video?**
   - Correct: Between 90 and 110 words.
3. **What is the function of the hook in the first 4 seconds?**
   - Correct: To capture viewer interest so they do not scroll away.

# Module 2: Creating Visuals and Audio

---

### Lesson 2.1: Generating Clean Scene Visuals with AI
- **Estimated Video Length**: 4 minutes 30 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-4`
- **Main Skill**: Prompt AI video and image tools for focused camera motion, realistic lighting, and stable subjects.
- **Prompting Camera Motion**: Use explicit cinematic directions like *"Slow smooth camera dolly-in, 4k cinematic lighting, shallow depth of field, steady focus"*. Avoid rapid erratic motions.

---

### Lesson 2.2: Maintaining Visual Consistency Across Scenes
- **Estimated Video Length**: 4 minutes 00 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-5`
- **Main Skill**: Keep lighting, color palette, and character details consistent across all scenes.
- **Consistency Technique**:
  - Keep a fixed style seed or use image-to-video reference.
  - Maintain the same color grade keywords across all scene prompts (e.g., *"warm amber lighting, cinematic natural contrast"*).

---

### Lesson 2.3: Generating Natural Voice Narration
- **Estimated Video Length**: 3 minutes 45 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-6`
- **Main Skill**: Convert script text into realistic speech with natural pacing, clear pronunciation, and proper pauses.
- **Tools**: ElevenLabs (Free tier) or CapCut native Text-to-Speech voices.
- **Tip**: Add commas and periods to force the voice AI to breathe and pause naturally between thoughts.

---

### Module 2 Quiz
1. **How can you prevent an AI voiceover from sounding rushed?**
   - Correct: Insert commas and periods strategically to create natural breathing pauses between thoughts.
2. **What technique helps keep video scenes visually consistent?**
   - Correct: Reusing consistent lighting, style descriptors, and image-to-video reference frames.

# Module 3: Assembly, Subtitling, and Packaging

---

### Lesson 3.1: Bringing Clips and Voice Together in CapCut
- **Estimated Video Length**: 4 minutes 45 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-7`
- **Main Skill**: Import audio and clips into CapCut, trim clips to beat cuts, and eliminate dead pauses.
- **Core Rule**: Cut on action. Never let a static scene linger on screen for more than 3 to 4 seconds in short-form video.

---

### Lesson 3.2: Adding Dynamic, Readable Subtitles
- **Estimated Video Length**: 3 minutes 30 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-8`
- **Main Skill**: Auto-generate subtitles in CapCut, style them for mobile visibility, and fix Nigerian proper noun spellings.
- **Formatting Checklist**:
  - Font: Bold, clean Sans-serif (e.g. Montserrat, Bold Canvas).
  - Size: Large enough to read on a 5-inch phone screen without squinting.
  - Position: Safe zone in middle-third (avoid bottom area where TikTok/Reels captions cover text).

---

### Lesson 3.3: Adding Sound Effects and Background Music Responsibly
- **Estimated Video Length**: 3 minutes 45 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-9`
- **Main Skill**: Layer subtle sound effects (whooshes, clicks) and balance background music levels.
- **Audio Balance Rule**:
  - Voiceover: 100% volume (0 dB).
  - Background Music: Lowered to 10% to 15% (-20 dB to -24 dB) so speech is crystal clear.

---

### Lesson 3.4: Reviewing Rights, Originality, and AI Content Disclosures
- **Estimated Video Length**: 4 minutes 00 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-10`
- **Main Skill**: Comply with YouTube, TikTok, and Meta AI transparency and synthetic media disclosure policies.
- **Disclosure Rule**: When uploading to YouTube or TikTok, toggle the "Altered or Synthetic Content" switch if realistic scenes were generated with AI. Never present AI characters as real human victims or witnesses.

---

### Module 3 Quiz
1. **Where should subtitles be positioned on vertical 9:16 videos?**

3. **What is required when uploading realistic AI-generated video on YouTube or TikTok?**
