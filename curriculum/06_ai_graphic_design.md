# Module 1: Design Fundamentals and Visual Hierarchy

---

### Lesson 1.1: The Purpose of Every Design: Information First
- **Estimated Video Length**: 3 minutes 30 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-1`
- **Main Skill**: Understand that commercial graphic design is visual communication that solves a problem, not decorative art.

#### 1. Video Production Plan & Timestamps
- **[0:00 - 0:25] Opening (Avatar on Screen)**
  - *Spoken script*: "Welcome to AI Graphic Design. Many beginners think good design means putting sparkles, ten different fonts, and five colors on a flyer. In this course, you will learn professional design: making information easy to read, beautiful, and convincing."
- **[0:25 - 1:00] Simple Explanation (Avatar + Comparison Graphic)**
  - *Visual*: Show a cluttered flyer on left (unreadable, rainbow colors) vs a clean, structured flyer on right.
  - *Spoken script*: "A flyer has only 3 seconds to catch someone's eye on a phone screen. If they cannot tell what you are selling, how much it costs, and who to contact, the flyer has failed. Design is communication first, decoration second."
- **[1:00 - 2:20] Live Demonstration (Screen Recording in Canva)**
  - *Screen*: Blank Canva canvas (1080x1080).
  - *Action*: Identify the 3 vital pieces of information:
    1. The Headline (What is happening?)
    2. The Key Detail (When, where, or how much?)
    3. The Action (Call or WhatsApp number).
  - *Voiceover*: "Notice what we put down first. Not shapes, not stickers. We place the three pieces of critical information in plain text. Everything we add later will serve these three facts."
- **[2:20 - 2:50] Result Review**
  - *Spoken script*: "When you start with information first, your designs stay clean, purposeful, and easy for clients to approve."
- **[2:50 - 3:15] Practice Task**
  - *Spoken script*: "Take a flyer from your neighborhood or WhatsApp status. Write down the 3 core facts it is trying to communicate."
- **[3:15 - 3:30] Next Lesson Preview**
  - *Spoken script*: "In Lesson 1.2, you will learn visual hierarchy: controlling what the viewer's eye sees first."

### Lesson 1.2: Visual Hierarchy: What the Eye Sees First, Second, and Last
- **Estimated Video Length**: 3 minutes 50 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-2`
- **Main Skill**: Structure layout size and contrast so the viewer reads information in intentional sequence.

#### 1. Video Production Plan & Timestamps
- **[0:00 - 0:25] Opening**: Explaining the concept of eye tracking and visual order.
- **[0:25 - 1:00] Simple Explanation**:
  - The Big (Headline): Seen first (e.g. 48px bold).
  - The Medium (Supporting Benefit / Date / Price): Seen second (e.g. 24px semi-bold).
  - The Small (Terms, address, contact badge): Seen third (e.g. 14px regular).
- **[1:00 - 2:30] Demonstration (Canva)**:
  - Take 3 sentences of identical font size.
  - Apply scale: make the offer huge, make the location medium, make the WhatsApp number clean and legible at bottom.
  - Show how the eye instantly glides from top to bottom.
- **[2:30 - 3:50] Practice Task & Next Lesson**: Rearrange 3 lines of text into clean hierarchy.

---

### Lesson 1.3: Typography and High-Contrast Readability
- **Estimated Video Length**: 3 minutes 45 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-3`
- **Main Skill**: Pair 2 legible fonts and maintain strong contrast against backgrounds.

#### 1. Video Production Plan & Timestamps
- **[0:00 - 0:55] Opening & Explanation**:
  - Rule 1: Never use more than 2 font families on a commercial flyer.
  - Rule 2: Dark text on light background, or light text on dark background. Never low-contrast gray on beige.
- **[0:55 - 2:30] Demonstration**:
  - Pair a clean bold Sans-serif header (e.g., Montserrat or League Spartan) with a neutral readable body font (e.g., Inter or Open Sans).
  - Test legibility by zooming out to 25% (simulating a mobile phone view).
- **[2:30 - 3:45] Result Review & Practice**: Choose 2 font pairings in Canva and test them on mobile.

---

### Module 1 Quiz
1. **What is the primary purpose of a commercial graphic design?**
   - Correct: To communicate information clearly and make the reader take action.
2. **How many font families should you typically combine on a commercial flyer?**
   - Correct: Maximum of 2 fonts (one for headline, one for body/details).
3. **How do you test if your design has good visual hierarchy?**
   - Correct: Zoom out to phone size and check if the eye immediately reads the headline first, then supporting details.

# Module 2: AI Visual Generation and Element Sourcing

---

### Lesson 2.1: Prompting AI for Specific Graphic Elements and Backgrounds
- **Estimated Video Length**: 4 minutes 15 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-4`
- **Main Skill**: Prompt image generators for isolated background textures, 3D icons, and realistic subjects without messy baked-in text.

#### 1. Video Production Plan & Timestamps
- **[0:00 - 0:50] Opening & Core Rule**: Never ask AI image generators to write text on your flyer. AI generates garbled, misspelled letters. Use AI only for the visual image, and add crisp text in Canva.
- **[0:50 - 2:40] Demonstration in Leonardo.ai / Recraft**:
  - Prompt:
    ```text
    Clean minimalist studio product pedestal, warm terracotta and cream geometric podium, soft morning sunlight, isolated on solid off-white background, commercial photography, high resolution, no text, no letters.
    ```
  - Show the result: a clean 3D podium ready for placing products.
- **[2:40 - 4:15] Review & Practice**: Prompt for a minimal coffee cup or organic cosmetic bottle on clean background.

---

### Lesson 2.2: Removing Backgrounds and Cleaning AI Artifacts
- **Estimated Video Length**: 3 minutes 30 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-5`
- **Main Skill**: Cut out AI-generated subjects cleanly using free background removal tools.
- **Tools**: Canva free background remover or `photoroom.com` / `remove.bg`.
- **Key Step**: Zoom in on edges (fingers, hair, bottle corners) to confirm the cutout does not look jagged or smudged.

---

### Lesson 2.3: Understanding Image Commercial Rights and Licenses
- **Estimated Video Length**: 3 minutes 15 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-6`
- **Main Skill**: Understand commercial licenses, copyright boundaries, and free credits terms.
- **Rules**:
  - Check the terms of service of the AI tool (Leonardo, Recraft, Midjourney) regarding commercial usage on free vs paid plans.
  - Never prompt using names of living individual artists to copy their exact style.
  - Do not use celebrity faces or trademarks (e.g. Nike swoosh, Apple logo) on client ads.

---

### Module 2 Quiz
1. **Why should you avoid letting AI generators type text on your flyer?**
   - Correct: AI image generators frequently misspell words and create garbled letters that look unprofessional.
2. **What should you add to your AI image prompts to prevent unwanted text?**
   - Correct: Negative instructions like "no text, no letters, no words".
3. **Can you legally put a celebrity's face or a trademarked logo on a client's commercial flyer without permission?**
   - Correct: No, using unauthorized celebrity images or brand trademarks infringes on legal personality and intellectual property rights.

### Module 3 Quiz
1. **What is the standard canvas dimension for a vertical Instagram feed post?**
   - Correct: 1080 x 1350 pixels (4:5 aspect ratio).

# Module 4: Polish, Export, and Portfolio

---

### Lesson 4.1: The 5-Point Quality Check: Spacing, Alignment, and Spelling
- **Estimated Video Length**: 3 minutes 30 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-10`
- **Main Skill**: Run a pre-flight quality inspection before sending any graphic to a client.
- **The 5-Point Checklist**:
  1. Phone numbers and spelling of addresses verified.
  2. Text aligned to a consistent margin (left-aligned or centered, never mixed randomly).
  3. Minimum font size on phone screen is legible (no critical text under 14px).
  4. Colors match the brand palette.
  5. Correct currency symbol used (₦ / $).

---

### Lesson 4.2: Choosing the Right File Formats and Sizes (PNG vs JPEG vs PDF)
- **Estimated Video Length**: 3 minutes 15 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-11`
- **Main Skill**: Export web-optimized, crisp files without blurry pixelation or bloated file sizes.
- **Format Guide**:
  - **PNG**: Best for social media, sharp text, and graphics with solid colors.
  - **JPEG**: Best for complex photography to keep file sizes light.
  - **PDF Print**: Best for flyers that will be taken to a physical printer.
