# PRODUCTION WORKFLOW & 8-SECOND AVATAR TIMELINE GUIDE
**For Google Flow AI Avatar + CapCut Mobile / Desktop Assembly**

---

### 1. The Pacing Formula (How the 8-Second Rule Works)
- **Natural conversational speed:** ~2.3 to 2.5 words per second.
- **8 Seconds = 18 to 22 words maximum.**
- Every clip in these scripts has been strictly timed and word-counted so your avatar never rushes or cuts off awkwardly.

---

### 2. Video Structure Template (2.5 – 3.5 Minutes Total per Video)

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [0:00 - 0:35]  AI AVATAR HOOK & CONCEPT                 │
│                (4 avatar clips × 8 seconds = 32s)       │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ [0:35 - 2:00]  YOUR MANUAL DEMO (Screen Recording)     │
│                - You record phone or laptop screen     │
│                - Type the exact prompt provided         │
│                - Show the AI response generating live   │
│                - Highlight key results & observations  │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ [2:00 - 2:40]  AI AVATAR PRACTICAL TAKEAWAY & OUTRO    │
│                (3-4 avatar clips × 8 seconds = ~30s)    │
│                - Summarize the golden rule             │
│                - Call to action & next lesson preview  │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

### 3. Assembly Checklist in CapCut (Batch Producing 12 Lessons in a Day)
1. **Batch generate the Avatar Clips:**
   - Feed the 8-second segments into your Google Flow AI avatar tool.
   - Export all clips with names like: `C1_L01_Clip1.mp4`, `C1_L01_Clip2.mp4`, etc.
2. **Screen Record your Demos:**
   - Keep your phone or laptop in 9:16 (vertical) or 16:9 (horizontal) matching your video format.
   - Copy-paste or type the provided prompts.
3. **Drop into CapCut:**
   - Line up Clips 1–4 -> Insert Screen Recording -> Line up Clips 5–6.
   - Auto-generate captions/subtitles in CapCut.
   - Keep background music volume at 10% to 15% (-22 dB) under speech.
   - Export 1080p.
