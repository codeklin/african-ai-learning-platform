  - *Visual*: Split screen. Left: avatar. Right: clean graphic `[Your Instruction] -> [AI Assistant] -> [Suggested Draft]`.
  - *Spoken script*: "Many people hear the word AI and think of robots in movies. In real life, the AI tools we use today are text assistants. Think of AI as a very fast reader that has read millions of books and web pages. When you type a sentence, it predicts the most helpful words that should come next. It does not think like a human. It follows patterns."
- **[0:50 - 2:15] Live Demonstration (Screen Recording)**
  - *Screen*: Mobile browser at `gemini.google.com` or `chatgpt.com`.
  - *Typed Prompt*:

  - *Action*: Send. Response appears word by word.

- **[2:15 - 2:40] Result Review (Screen Highlight)**
  - *Visual*: Highlight:
    > *"A savings account is a secure bank account where you keep money you do not plan to spend immediately. It helps protect your money while earning a small amount of extra interest over time."*
  - *Spoken script*: "Look at the result. It is clear, simple, and directly answers what we asked. That is the core job of an AI assistant: turning a request into a helpful draft."
- **[2:40 - 3:05] Practice Task**
  - *Spoken script*: "Now it is your turn. Open a free AI assistant on your phone or laptop. Ask it to explain what an invoice is in two simple sentences. Read the answer to see if it makes sense."
- **[3:05 - 3:15] Next Lesson Preview**
  - *Spoken script*: "In the next lesson, we will look at what AI does well and the common mistakes it makes. See you there."

#### 2. Copyable Prompt
```text
Explain what a savings account is in two simple sentences for a student.
```

#### 3. Practice Task & Expected Result
- **Task**: Open ChatGPT or Google Gemini. Type a prompt asking the AI to explain what an invoice is in two simple sentences for a new trader.
- **Expected Result**: A clean, two-sentence response explaining that an invoice is a bill sent to a buyer listing goods or services supplied and the amount to be paid.
- **Common Mistake**: Typing only one word like "Invoice". The AI will not know if you want a definition, a template, or a story. Always give a specific command.

#### 4. Notes for the Video Editor
- Do not use background music with heavy bass or lyrics.
- Zoom in 150% on the chat input box when typing the prompt so mobile viewers can read every letter clearly.
- Highlight the prompt and the response using a subtle warm terracotta outline (`#E86A58`).

#### 5. Downloadable Text Lesson Notes
- Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools like ChatGPT or Gemini are text assistants.
- They work by predicting the best words to follow your instruction based on patterns.
- AI does not know your personal situation unless you describe it.
- Always give AI a clear instruction instead of typing a single keyword.

### Lesson 1.1: The Purpose of Your Video and Who It Is For
- **Estimated Video Length**: 3 minutes 30 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-1`
- **Main Skill**: Identify a clear video objective and target audience before generating any AI clips.
- **Key Principle**: Never generate random pretty videos. Pick one specific question to answer or one story to tell.

---

### Lesson 1.2: Writing a Short 45-Second Hook-and-Hold Script
- **Estimated Video Length**: 4 minutes 15 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-2`
- **Main Skill**: Structure a 3-part script optimized for vertical retention (Shorts / Reels / TikTok).
- **The 45-Second Script Structure**:
  1. The Hook (0–4 seconds / ~10 words): Stop the thumb scroll.
  2. The Core Insight (5–35 seconds / ~60 words): Deliver the value in 3 distinct beats.
  3. The Takeaway & CTA (36–45 seconds / ~20 words): One clear lesson and action.
- **Copyable Script Prompt**:
  ```text
  Act as a short-form video scriptwriter for TikTok and Instagram Reels.
  Topic: 3 simple daily habits that protect your laptop battery in hot Nigerian weather.
  Target audience: Students and remote workers.
  Write a word-for-word spoken script that takes exactly 45 seconds to speak (around 90 to 100 words total).
  Structure:
  - Hook (0-5s): A punchy opening that calls out overheating laptops.
  - Beat 1, 2, 3: Practical steps (avoid bedsheets, charge between 20-80%, keep out of direct car sunlight).
  - Outro (40-45s): Follow for practical tech tips.
  Do not include stage directions in the dialogue text.
  ```

---

### Lesson 1.3: Storyboarding and Scene Planning
- **Estimated Video Length**: 3 minutes 45 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-3`
- **Main Skill**: Break a script into 5 to 7 specific visual scenes before opening an AI generator.
- **Storyboard Template**:
  - Scene 1 (0–5s): Close-up shot of laptop vents blocked by a bedsheet.
  - Scene 2 (5–15s): Overheating battery indicator warning on a phone/laptop screen.
  - Scene 3 (15–25s): Laptop sitting elevated on a clean wooden desk with airflow.
  - Scene 4 (25–35s): Battery gauge showing 80% charging pause.
  - Scene 5 (35–45s): Smiling creator working smoothly at a desk.

- **Audio Balance Rule**:
  - Voiceover: 100% volume (0 dB).

### Lesson 4.1: The 5-Point Quality Check: Spacing, Alignment, and Spelling
- **Estimated Video Length**: 3 minutes 30 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-10`
- **Main Skill**: Run a pre-flight quality inspection before sending any graphic to a client.
- **The 5-Point Checklist**:
  1. Phone numbers and spelling of addresses verified.
  2. Text aligned to a consistent margin (left-aligned or centered, never mixed randomly).
  3. Minimum font size on phone screen is legible (no critical text under 14px).
  4. Colors match the brand palette.
  5. Correct currency symbol used (₦ / $).

### Lesson 4.2: Choosing the Right File Formats and Sizes (PNG vs JPEG vs PDF)
- **Estimated Video Length**: 3 minutes 15 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-11`
- **Main Skill**: Export web-optimized, crisp files without blurry pixelation or bloated file sizes.
- **Format Guide**:
  - **PNG**: Best for social media, sharp text, and graphics with solid colors.
  - **JPEG**: Best for complex photography to keep file sizes light.
  - **PDF Print**: Best for flyers that will be taken to a physical printer.
