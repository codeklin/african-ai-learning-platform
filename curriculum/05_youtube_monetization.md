# Module 1: Channel Foundations and Audience Value

---

### Lesson 1.1: How the YouTube Platform Actually Works
- **Estimated Video Length**: 4 minutes 00 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-1`
- **Main Skill**: Understand search intent, recommendation algorithms, Click-Through Rate (CTR), and Average View Duration (AVD).
- **Core Principle**: YouTube's algorithm does not push videos; viewers pull videos. The algorithm simply follows what viewers click and enjoy watching.

---

### Lesson 1.2: Choosing a Specific Channel Topic and Audience Promise
- **Estimated Video Length**: 3 minutes 45 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-2`
- **Main Skill**: Define a clear, focused channel proposition that gives viewers a reason to subscribe.
- **The Channel Proposition Formula**:
  > *"I help [specific audience] learn or achieve [specific outcome] through [practical, original content]."*

---

### Lesson 1.3: Researching Demand and Validating Video Topics
- **Estimated Video Length**: 4 minutes 15 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-3`
- **Main Skill**: Validate topic interest using YouTube search auto-suggest, competitor analysis, and viewer question gaps.
- **Prompt**:
  ```text
  Act as a YouTube audience researcher.
  Channel topic: Practical personal finance and small business operations for Nigerian traders and freelancers.
  List 10 specific questions people are actively searching on YouTube about this topic.
  Group them into 3 categories: Beginner questions, Common mistakes, and Tool tutorials.
  ```

---

### Module 1 Quiz
1. **What drives video distribution on YouTube?**
   - Correct: Viewer satisfaction, evidenced by Click-Through Rate (CTR) and Average View Duration (retention).
2. **Why should a new YouTube channel focus on one clear topic?**
   - Correct: It helps YouTube understand who to recommend the videos to, and gives subscribers a clear reason to return.
3. **Does buying views or subscribers help your YouTube channel grow?**
   - Correct: No, artificial engagement violates YouTube policy and destroys the algorithm's ability to find real viewers.

# Module 2: Video Ideation, Scripting, and Packaging

---

### Lesson 2.1: Crafting Titles That Spark Curiosity Honestly
- **Estimated Video Length**: 3 minutes 45 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-4`
- **Main Skill**: Write titles that generate genuine curiosity without misleading viewers (clickbait).
- **Rule**: Your video must deliver on every promise made in the title within the first 60 seconds.

---

### Lesson 2.2: Designing High-Contrast, Legible Mobile Thumbnails
- **Estimated Video Length**: 4 minutes 30 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-5`
- **Main Skill**: Design thumbnails in Canva that are instantly legible on small phone screens.
- **The Rule of Three Elements**: A great mobile thumbnail contains maximum 3 visual elements:
  1. A clear facial expression or primary object.
  2. A high-contrast background.
  3. Maximum 3 to 4 bold words (or zero words).

---

### Lesson 2.3: Writing Video Scripts That Retain Viewers
- **Estimated Video Length**: 4 minutes 45 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-6`
- **Main Skill**: Script a structured 5- to 8-minute video that hooks viewers, eliminates fluff, and keeps engagement high.
- **Script Structure**:
  - The Hook (0–30s): Confirm the title promise immediately.
  - The Meat (30s–6m): Deliver 3 actionable points with visual examples.
  - The Payoff (6m–7m): Final synthesis and recommendation for the next video to watch.

---

### Module 2 Quiz
1. **What is the maximum number of words recommended on a mobile YouTube thumbnail?**
   - Correct: 3 to 4 bold words, keeping text minimal and legible.
2. **Why should you confirm the title promise within the first 30 seconds of the video?**
   - Correct: Because viewers click away immediately if they feel the video is taking too long to get to the point.

# Module 3: Policies, Copyright, and Synthetic Content Rules

---

### Lesson 3.1: The YouTube Partner Program (YPP) Requirements
- **Estimated Video Length**: 3 minutes 30 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-7`
- **Main Skill**: Understand official thresholds (e.g. 1,000 subscribers, 4,000 watch hours or 10M Shorts views), AdSense verification, and country availability.
- **Policy Verification**: Always verify current thresholds directly on the official YouTube Help Center.

---

### Lesson 3.2: Copyright, Fair Use, and Avoiding Content Strikes
- **Estimated Video Length**: 4 minutes 15 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-8`
- **Main Skill**: Understand copyright protection, fair use boundaries, and sourcing music from YouTube Audio Library.
- **Warning**: Using popular commercial songs without a license will lead to Content ID claims or copyright strikes.

---

### Lesson 3.3: Reused Content and Inauthentic/Spam Policies
- **Estimated Video Length**: 4 minutes 30 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-9`
- **Main Skill**: Understand why auto-generated, repetitive, or mass-produced AI videos get rejected for monetization under YouTube's Reused Content policy.
- **Key Requirement**: Human editorial commentary, genuine original value, or educational transformation must be clearly present.

---

### Lesson 3.4: Disclosing AI-Generated and Altered Content
- **Estimated Video Length**: 3 minutes 45 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-10`
- **Main Skill**: Learn when and how to select the "Altered content" checkbox in YouTube Studio when uploading AI-generated media.

---

### Module 3 Quiz
1. **Can you monetize a channel that simply re-uploads clips from movies or other creators without commentary?**
   - Correct: No, this violates YouTube's Reused Content policy and will be rejected.
2. **Where can you find free, copyright-safe music for your YouTube videos?**
   - Correct: The official YouTube Studio Audio Library.
3. **What happens if you hide that your realistic video was made using AI tools?**
   - Correct: You risk penalties, demonetization, or content removal under YouTube's synthetic media policies.
