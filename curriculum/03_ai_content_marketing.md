# Module 1: Brief Analysis and Customer Research

---

### Lesson 1.1: Dissecting a Real Business Brief
- **Estimated Video Length**: 3 minutes 45 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-1`
- **Main Skill**: Extract the core target audience, key offer, business objective, and tone from a messy client brief.
- **The 4-Part Brief Framework**:
  1. Target Customer (Who are they? What is their daily routine?)
  2. Core Offer (What product or service are they purchasing?)
  3. Business Goal (What specific action should they take?)
  4. Brand Tone (Professional, friendly, playful, or luxury?)

---

### Lesson 1.2: Uncovering Real Customer Questions and Pain Points with AI
- **Estimated Video Length**: 4 minutes 00 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-2`
- **Main Skill**: Prompt an AI assistant to identify customer objections, desires, and daily concerns.
- **Prompt**:
  ```text
  Act as a senior consumer research specialist in Nigeria.
  Target audience: Professional women aged 25 to 40 working in corporate offices in Lagos.
  Product: Ready-to-wear corporate work dresses (₦25,000 to ₦45,000).
  List 5 specific daily frustrations they experience when buying work clothes, and 5 common hesitations they have before ordering clothes online.
  Format your answer as two clean bullet lists. Use simple, direct language.
  ```

---

### Lesson 1.3: Setting Content Pillars for a Business
- **Estimated Video Length**: 3 minutes 30 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-3`
- **Main Skill**: Establish 3 to 4 recurring content themes that balance education, trust-building, and direct sales.
- **The 3 Pillars**:
  1. Educate / Problem Solving (40%)
  2. Trust & Social Proof (30%)
  3. Direct Offers with Price & CTA (30%)

---

### Module 1 Quiz
1. **Why is it dangerous to start writing content before breaking down the client brief?**
   - Correct: Because without knowing the specific audience and business goal, your content will lack direction and fail to drive action.
2. **What is a "content pillar"?**
   - Correct: A recurring core theme that guides your topics, balancing helpfulness, trust, and sales.
3. **What is the problem with posting "Buy now!" messages every day?**
   - Correct: It exhausts audience attention and provides no value or proof before asking for money.

# Module 2: Drafting Social Media and Ad Copy

---

### Lesson 2.1: Writing Hook-Driven Instagram & LinkedIn Captions
- **Estimated Video Length**: 4 minutes 15 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-4`
- **Main Skill**: Write social media captions with an attention-grabbing first line, relatable body copy, and a single clear call to action.
- **Prompt**:
  ```text
  Act as a direct-response social media copywriter.
  Business: Ready-to-wear corporate fashion brand for women in Lagos.
  Topic: Crepe work dresses that stay neat all day without wrinkling during commutes.
  Write an Instagram caption following this structure:
  1. Hook: One punchy sentence that speaks to women sitting in morning traffic worried about wrinkled clothes.
  2. Body: 3 short paragraphs (max 2 sentences each) explaining our wrinkle-free fabric and breathable cut.
  3. Call to Action: Tell them to tap the link in bio to view available sizes on WhatsApp.
  Include 3 relevant hashtags. Do not use generic words like "elevate your wardrobe".
  ```

---

### Lesson 2.2: Crafting WhatsApp Broadcasts and Status Updates
- **Estimated Video Length**: 3 minutes 30 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-5`
- **Main Skill**: Write conversational, respectful WhatsApp broadcasts and status slides that convert readers into buyers.
- **Prompt**:
  ```text
  Write a 3-part WhatsApp status update series for a catering service taking weekend small chops orders.
  Slide 1: Problem / situation (Friday gatherings, office weekend parties).
  Slide 2: The offer (50-piece fresh small chops box, ₦12,500, delivered warm).
  Slide 3: Clear ordering deadline (Book before 2:00 PM Thursday for Saturday delivery).
  Keep each slide under 25 words so it fits comfortably on a WhatsApp status image or text card.
  ```

---

### Lesson 2.3: Writing Short-Form Promotional and Ad Copy
- **Estimated Video Length**: 3 minutes 50 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-6`
- **Main Skill**: Write concise ad copy for Meta/Instagram ads that communicates clear value without triggering policy rejections.
- **Prompt**:
  ```text
  Act as a Meta ads specialist for African small businesses.
  Business: Professional CV and LinkedIn revamp service for job seekers in Nigeria.
  Price: ₦15,000.
  Write 2 short ad copy variants (under 60 words each):
  - Variant A: Focus on saving time and feeling confident when applying.
  - Variant B: Focus on past clients landing interviews after fixing poorly formatted CVs.
  Do not guarantee jobs or income. Use clear, compliant wording.
  Include a headline and a primary text for each variant.
  ```

---

### Module 2 Quiz
1. **What is the primary job of the first line of an Instagram caption?**
   - Correct: To stop the viewer from scrolling and compel them to tap 'more'.
2. **Why should you state clear prices in WhatsApp status updates or broadcasts?**
   - Correct: Because transparent pricing filters out unqualified inquiries and respects buyers' time.
3. **What kind of claims will get your Meta ad account flagged or banned?**
   - Correct: Exaggerated or guaranteed income, quick weight loss, or unrealistic promises.

# Module 3: Email Campaigns and Brand Consistency

---

### Lesson 3.1: Writing Welcome and Follow-Up Email Sequences
- **Estimated Video Length**: 4 minutes 15 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-7`
- **Main Skill**: Structure and write a 3-part automated email welcome and nurture sequence.
- **Prompt**:
  ```text
  Act as an email copywriter for a digital skills training platform in Nigeria.
  Lead magnet: Free 5-page Guide on "How to Invoice Foreign Clients from Nigeria".
  Write a 3-email sequence:
  Email 1: Thank them, deliver the guide link, and explain what to read first (under 120 words).
  Email 2: Share one common invoicing mistake that delays payments (under 150 words).
  Email 3: Introduce our Paid Freelance Service Course with a special 20% discount code (under 150 words).
  Include an engaging subject line for each email. Use warm, professional language.
  ```

---

### Lesson 3.2: Training AI on a Specific Brand Voice
- **Estimated Video Length**: 3 minutes 45 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-8`
- **Main Skill**: Feed existing brand samples into an AI assistant so it matches vocabulary, tone, and formatting rules.
- **Rule**: Save 2–3 best past client samples and paste them at the start of prompting sessions to ground the AI in authentic tone.

---

### Lesson 3.3: The Quality and Fact-Checking Filter
- **Estimated Video Length**: 3 minutes 30 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-9`
- **Main Skill**: Review campaign copy for legal compliance, factual accuracy, image rights, and authenticity.
- **Rule**: Replace impossible guarantees with specific, honest statements. Add delivery boundaries upfront.

---

### Module 3 Quiz
1. **What is the recommended structure for a 3-part email welcome sequence?**
   - Correct: Email 1: Deliver promised resource; Email 2: Share practical value/story; Email 3: Present clear offer.
2. **How do you make an AI assistant write in a specific brand's voice?**
   - Correct: Paste 2 to 3 real writing samples from the brand and specify words or tones to avoid.
3. **Why must you clarify terms like "Free Delivery" in promotional copy?**
   - Correct: Because customers will be upset and feel deceived if they discover hidden restrictions at checkout.

# Module 4: Content Workflow and Client Portfolio

### Lesson 4.1: Building a 7-Day Content Calendar
- **Estimated Video Length**: 4 minutes 00 seconds
- **Lesson Slug**: `lesson-10`
- **Main Skill**: Plan and organize a 7-day multi-channel publishing schedule in a spreadsheet.
- **Prompt**:
  ```text
  Act as a social media content strategist for a Nigerian skincare brand.
  Pillars:
  1. Skin Health Education (Mon & Thu)
  2. Customer Reviews & Social Proof (Tue & Sat)
  3. Direct Offers on WhatsApp (Wed & Fri)
  4. Behind-the-Scenes & Routine (Sun)

  Create a 7-day content calendar table with these columns:
  1. Day (Monday - Sunday)
  2. Platform (Instagram / WhatsApp Status)
  3. Pillar
  4. Topic & Angle (1 clear sentence)
  5. Call to Action
  Keep it practical, realistic, and tailored to busy customers.
  ```
