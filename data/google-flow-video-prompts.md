# ItuwaAfrica: Google Flow (Veo 2) Marketing Video Prompts

## How to Use These Prompts

1. Go to labs.google/flow and sign in with your Google account.
2. Click "New video" or the generate button.
3. Copy one prompt block below and paste it into the prompt field.
4. Set aspect ratio to 9:16 (vertical) for TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels.
   Set aspect ratio to 16:9 (horizontal) for YouTube long-form thumbnails or ads.
5. Tap Generate. The free tier gives you a limited number of clips per day.
6. Download the best clip. String multiple clips together in CapCut to build a full ad.

## Free Tier Tips

- Each generation produces one 5 to 8 second clip. Plan for 4 to 6 clips per video.
- If a result is blurry or off-target, change one descriptive word and regenerate.
- Use "cinematic, shallow depth of field, warm natural light" in every prompt for a consistent look.
- Avoid prompts with more than two people in the same scene. The model handles single subjects best.
- Always include "9:16 vertical frame" in the prompt so the composition is correct for mobile.
- Save every clip you like immediately. Downloads expire after a session on the free tier.
