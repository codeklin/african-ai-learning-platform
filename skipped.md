# Course lineup and purchase notes

The initial course lineup is confirmed:

- **Free:** AI Foundations.
- **Paid, one-time purchase:** AI Video Creation : USD 4; AI Web App Development : NGN 50,000; YouTube Monetization : NGN 50,000; AI Graphic Design : NGN 50,000.

Paid courses may be pre-ordered while their content is in development. Learners must be told that no release date is promised and that lessons unlock only after publication and manual payment verification.

The purchase panel uses ExchangeRate-API Open Access (`https://open.er-api.com/v6/latest/USD`) for daily USD/NGN equivalents. The owner supplied NGN 1,400 per USD as a reference, not a guaranteed rate. Cache up to 24 hours, validate the response, display the rate timestamp and attribution, and never substitute the reference rate if the live rate is unavailable.

Payment is by NGN bank transfer. Account and WhatsApp details were supplied by the owner but are intentionally not stored in source control; configure them as server-side environment variables. Learners share transfer references over WhatsApp. An admin must confirm the bank credit before recording an enrollment. Do not treat a receipt or learner message as verification.

Cancellation and refund terms, and course release dates, have not been set. Avoid promising either; direct learners to confirm applicable terms before transferring. For YouTube Monetization, policy-dependent lessons must include an official source, last-reviewed date, and review status before publication.
